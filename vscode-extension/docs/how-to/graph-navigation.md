Use the graph view to explore relationships across your workspace.

## Steps

1. Open the Graph panel from the Activity Bar.
2. Pan and zoom to explore clusters of linked notes.
3. Click a node to focus it and reveal its direct connections.
4. Use the search input to locate a specific note.

## Tips

- Large workspaces render faster after a full index rebuild.
- Use `linkity.excludePatterns` to remove noisy directories from the graph.
